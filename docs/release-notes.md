Alpha build. Bring your own copy of the game: nothing in these packages contains game data.

## What's new: local co-op

- **Single camera Coop** (party members on extra controllers). With one instance, press **Start** on a second, third or fourth gamepad to take
  control of the next free party member (left stick moves, A attacks, B jumps, Y picks up, right stick or R + left/right
  chooses a spell, hold X casts). **Hold Back for about ¾ s** to give the member back to the AI.
- **Dynamic camera.** With extra controllers taking party members in a single offline instance, the camera zooms out
  (never closer than your camera distance setting, up to 400%) and keeps everyone in view. Wireless and online games use
  the camera distance setting instead.
- **Camera distance setting** from 50% to 400%, with the 3D flicker at high zoom fixed.
- **Party members use map exits** and entrances, taking everyone with them.
- **Party members interact**: they can talk to NPCs, open shops and use crystals.
- **All controllers use menus**: any gamepad can advance dialogue, skip cutscenes and use menus.
- **Parallel instances** (Windows): run 2-4 copies of the game (or `--players <n>`); closing the settings after changing
  the number restarts the game. Only player 1 has music and cutscene sound.
- **One window for all instances** (on by default): every instance is shown in a quarter of a single window. Good for the
  Steam Deck and single-screen setups. Turn it off for one window per instance.
- **Select** shows or hides the bottom screen in each player's quarter.

See the README's "Local co-op" section for setup steps.
