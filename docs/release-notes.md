Alpha build. Bring your own copy of the game: nothing in these packages contains game data.

## What's new: local co-op

- **Party members on extra controllers.** With one window, press **Start** on a second, third or fourth gamepad to take
  control of the next free party member (left stick moves, A attacks, B jumps, Y picks up, right stick or R + left/right
  chooses a spell, hold X casts). **Hold Back for about ¾ s** to give the member back to the AI.
- **Dynamic camera.** With more than one player the camera zooms out (never closer than your camera distance setting, up
  to 400%) and keeps everyone in view.
- **Camera distance setting** from 50% to 400%, with the 3D flicker at high zoom fixed.
- **Party members use map exits** and entrances, taking everyone with them.
- **All controllers use menus**: any gamepad can advance dialogue, skip cutscenes and use menus.
- **One window for all players** (Windows, on by default): with Local players set to 2-4 (or `--players <n>`), every
  player is shown in a quarter of a single window. Good for the Steam Deck and single-screen setups. Turn it off for one
  window per player.
- **Select** shows or hides the bottom screen in each player's quarter.

Experimental: party members talking to NPCs and opening shops (WIP, off by default). The shared window has not been tested
under Proton or on a Steam Deck yet. See the README's "Local co-op" section for setup steps.
